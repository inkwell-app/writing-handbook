# Choose and adapt a template

Start with the smallest artifact that answers your next question. You do not need to fill in every table before writing.

| Situation | Start with | Add only when needed | Useful first entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| A story idea has no shape yet | [Project brief](../templates/project-brief.md) | [Outline](../templates/outline.md) | “A two-page scene in which a departure becomes a decision to stay.” |
| A long draft loses facts across chapters | [Continuity ledger](../templates/continuity-ledger.md) | Revision log | “Mara already knows the battery is in her bag; later discovery cannot surprise her.” |
| Feedback arrives from several readers | [Revision log](../templates/revision-log.md) | [Feedback session](../templates/feedback-session.md) | Separate “I lost the timeline” from “move the flashback.” |
| A research claim needs checking | [Evidence ledger](../templates/evidence-ledger.md) | Continuity ledger for dependent conclusions | Record the exact claim, source passage, date and limits together. |
| A team needs to make a decision | Project brief | Evidence ledger and revision log | Name the decision-maker, options and what evidence would change the recommendation. |
| Someone else needs to edit or present your work | [Handoff manifest](../templates/handoff-manifest.md) | Revision log for unresolved questions | Name the source draft and what the recipient must do with the export. |

## A ten-minute first pass

1. Copy one template to your own working folder. Give it a project name.
2. Fill in the project, purpose and one real row. Leave irrelevant columns out.
3. Use stable IDs for items that other documents refer to: `C01` for a continuity fact, `R01` for a revision, `E01` for evidence.
4. Test the row against the actual draft. Could another reader find the passage and understand the decision?
5. Add detail only when it prevents a misunderstanding. An unresolved question with an owner is more useful than a confidently filled empty field.

## Adapt to your writing

For a screenplay, an outline row may be a scene and its change in dramatic state. For a novel, use a chapter or beat and track point of view. For a research document, use a section and the claim it establishes. For a team decision, use a question, the options considered and the evidence needed to choose.

Treat priority as a decision, not an automatic score: fix a missing premise before polishing a paragraph that depends on it. Use `Open`, `In progress`, `Verified` and `Declined` consistently in a revision log. A declined suggestion should retain its reason so the next review does not repeat it.

- [The Last Light](../examples/the-last-light.md): scene setup, a rejected AI edit, continuity and verification.
