# Completed example: The Last Light

Original fictional exercise created for this handbook. All characters and events are invented.

## Brief

A two-page scene for a workshop. Mara must decide whether to leave a lighthouse before the storm reaches the harbor. The audience should understand her choice without an explanatory speech. Done means the decision is visible in action and the final beat follows from an earlier setup.

## Outline

| ID | Unit | Opening state | Change | Ending state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Lantern room | Mara plans to leave | A boat’s signal appears offshore | She postpones leaving |
| 02 | Stairwell | The lamp is failing | She retrieves the spare battery she packed | She chooses to return upstairs |

## Revision log

| ID | Observation | Decision | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | The spare battery appears without setup | Show Mara packing it before she sees the boat | Read both scenes: the same bag and battery appear in each |
| R02 | A speech explains the choice the action already shows | Replace the speech with Mara setting down her coat | Ask a reader what she has decided and which action shows it |

## Continuity ledger

| Fact | Established | Depends on it | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mara carries the spare battery | Opening action in scene 01 | Retrieval in scene 02 | Keep the bag with her when she moves downstairs |

## Handoff

Send the workshop a PDF for reading and keep the editable source. Name the draft `last-light-workshop-02`; check both pages after export. This naming example is a workflow choice, not an Inkwell filename requirement.

## Read the actual revision

### Baseline excerpt

```text
INT. LIGHTHOUSE LANTERN ROOM - NIGHT

Mara folds her coat over one arm. Offshore, a lamp flashes twice.
The lighthouse beam dies.

MARA
I have to stay. Someone needs the light.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE STAIRWELL - NIGHT

Mara opens her bag and discovers a spare battery. She runs upstairs.
```

The choice is explained in speech, but the repair depends on an object the reader has never seen. “Discovers” also suggests Mara did not know what she packed.

### Revised excerpt

```text
INT. LIGHTHOUSE LANTERN ROOM - NIGHT

Mara slips a spare battery into her bag and lifts her coat.
Offshore, a lamp flashes twice. The lighthouse beam dies.

She sets the coat on the floor.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE STAIRWELL - NIGHT

Mara stops on the first step. She takes the battery from her bag,
turns, and climbs back toward the lantern room.
```

### Decisions and checks

| Suggestion | Decision | Reason | Check against the revised text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Establish the battery before the failure | Accepted | The repair now has visible setup | Battery enters the bag before the beam dies |
| Let an action carry the decision | Accepted | The coat makes the interruption visible | Mara puts the coat down rather than leaving with it |
| Add “Mara has always feared the sea” | Declined | It invents a backstory the scene does not establish | No fear-of-the-sea claim was introduced |
| Replace the bag with a wall cabinet | Declined for this pass | It changes the existing prop dependency | The same bag appears in both scenes |

Try reviewing the two excerpts without this table. Locate the setup, the decision and the prop dependency. Then propose an alternative action that conveys the same choice without adding a new fact. A reader may still find the signal unclear; that is a question for the next workshop, not something this edit proves solved.
