# Review a revision

1. Preserve the baseline and name the purpose of this revision: structure, continuity, clarity or line work.
2. Collect feedback in the [revision log](../templates/revision-log.md). Separate each observation from the proposed fix.
3. Resolve structural decisions before polishing text that may move or disappear.
4. Check affected facts and dependencies in the [continuity ledger](../templates/continuity-ledger.md).
5. Compare the new draft against the baseline. Explain intentional changes and investigate accidental ones.
6. Close verified tasks, retain unresolved decisions, and label the new draft for the next reader.

**Finished when:** every accepted change has a visible result and every unresolved issue has an owner or a documented decision.

**In Inkwell:** named save points help mark review milestones; draft lines let you explore an alternative. Use the website’s product help for the exact controls. The [completed example](../examples/the-last-light.md) shows a small revision from problem to verification.
