# Review AI edits

1. Save a baseline and state the editing goal in one sentence. List facts, voice choices and story outcomes that must remain.
2. Give the assistant only material you are authorized to share. Review the tool’s current data-handling terms before providing sensitive work.
3. Review each suggested change in context. A smoother sentence can still remove a clue, alter a promise or invent evidence.
4. Check every new factual claim, quotation and citation against the original source using the [evidence ledger](../templates/evidence-ledger.md).
5. Read surrounding scenes or sections and check the [continuity ledger](../templates/continuity-ledger.md).
6. Accept, revise or reject suggestions deliberately. Record substantive decisions in the [revision log](../templates/revision-log.md).

**Finished when:** the draft meets the stated goal without losing required meaning. Model confidence is not verification. This is a human review workflow, not an automated quality score.

**Further practice:** the [research directory](../README.md#open-tools-and-research) includes synthetic screenplay revision tasks. Their dataset card explains their scope; they do not establish quality across all writing.
